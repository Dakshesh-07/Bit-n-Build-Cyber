## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a dual mandate: unshakeable authority and nurturing calm. Built for modern youth protection, digital monitoring, and child-safe environments, the interface balances clear functional warnings with serene, stress-reducing domesticity.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Warmth** and **Modern Functionalism**. High-tension situations (such as geofence breaches or safety overrides) demand absolute clarity, while daily parental and institutional interactions require an approachable, non-intrusive environment. The canvas rejects cold, clinical whites and utilitarian grays in favor of sand-washed, grounded stone tones paired with structured deep charcoal blues. Safety amber provides immediate, purposeful focus without triggering unnecessary panic.

### Design Principles
- **Vigilance Without Anxiety:** Use soft, natural sand foundations to ground the user, reserving high-contrast deep blue and amber strictly for functional hierarchy and vital telemetry.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts favor clear card structures, definite boundaries, and predictable interaction models to instill immediate trust.
- **Protective Ergonomics:** Touch targets are oversized, tap boundaries are distinct, and interactive states respond with decisive, physical immediacy.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, dependable ecosystem using warm mineral bases, authoritative deep charcoal blue, and an unmistakable safety amber signal.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#182232`):** Deep Charcoal Blue. Represents institutional integrity, stability, and primary interactive hierarchy. Used for high-emphasis buttons, active navigation states, primary headings, and critical bounding strokes.
- **Secondary (`#f59e0b`):** Safety Amber. Serves as the primary functional alert and cautionary indicator. Used for warning badges, immediate awareness tags, live child status shifts, and contextual highlight calls.
- **Secondary Dark (`#d97706`):** Deep Safety Amber. Reserved for hover states, active press feedback, and small-format text warnings requiring WCAG AA compliance against sand backgrounds.
- **Secondary Container (`#fef3c7`):** Pale Amber Tint. Used for background fills on warning callouts, notification ribbons, and geofence caution zones.
- **Tertiary (`#2a4365`):** Slate Marine. Used for supportive navigational items, secondary actions, informational icons, and chart accents.
- **Neutral & Surfaces:**
  - **Canvas Base (`#f7f5f0`):** Warm alabaster sand. Creates a comforting, non-glare surface for all screens.
  - **Surface Default (`#ffffff`):** Pure white. Applied exclusively to floating cards, active panels, and dialogs to elevate content off the sand canvas.
  - **Surface Muted (`#efece4`):** Pressed stone sand. Used for container recesses, segmented controls, table headers, and structural dividing rules.
  - **Border Base (`#ded9cc`):** Low-contrast warm gray-sand boundary for tactile structural definition.
  - **Text Neutral Primary (`#0f172a`):** Maximum-contrast midnight charcoal for body and heading text.
  - **Text Neutral Muted (`#64748b`):** Balanced slate for metadata, captions, and secondary timestamps.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** provides geometric precision softened by friendly, human apertures. It balances administrative gravitas with accessible warmth, making high-stress monitoring dashboards feel protective rather than cold or punitive.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Set with tight tracking (`-0.02em`) and bold weights to project firm confidence and high clarity during safety status checks.
- **Body Copies:** Regular weight at 15px with a relaxed 24px line height ensures long-form activity logs and family care notes remain effortless to parse in direct sunlight or dim conditions.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** `label-sm` utilizes uppercase rendering with `0.04em` tracking for real-time telemetry tags, device statuses (e.g., `ONLINE`, `BATTERY CRITICAL`, `ZONE LEFT`), and category markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a disciplined fluid grid configured to support quick visual scanning across responsive breakpoints.

### Breakpoints & Grid Rules
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` page margins. Max layout width constrained to `1280px` for optimal scanning.
- **Tablet (640px to 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Primary metrics span 4 columns; map and tracking panels span 8 columns.
- **Mobile (Up to 639px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` outer canvas padding. Stacked single-column layouts for alerts and child cards.

### Spacing Discipline
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro offsets between icons and adjacent text labels.
- `space-sm` (8px): Gaps within input groups, chip sets, and badge internals.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard internal padding for cards, lists, and stacked field groups.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card-to-card gaps, modal inner padding, and section division.
- `space-xl` (40px): Major block breaks between telemetry zones and control sets.

## Elevation & Depth

This system eschews stark digital drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Grounding** supplemented by warm ambient diffusion. Surfaces feel like physical cards resting softly upon a warm sand table.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Floor (Canvas):** `#f7f5f0` (Alabaster Sand). No shadow, completely flat ground plane.
2. **Layer 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** `#ffffff` with a subtle composite shadow:
   - `0 1px 3px rgba(24, 34, 50, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(222, 217, 204, 0.4)`
   - Border: `1px solid #ded9cc`
3. **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Elements, Dropdowns, Sheets):** `#ffffff` with elevated warmth:
   - `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(24, 34, 50, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 34, 50, 0.04)`
   - Border: `1px solid #d5cec0`
4. **Layer 3 (Modals & Emergency Overlays):** `#ffffff` with high-emphasis containment:
   - `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`
   - Backdrop: `rgba(24, 34, 50, 0.45)` with `4px` background blur.

### Safety Amber Glow
Critical alert cards and active geofence warnings project a dedicated ambient luminescence:
- `0 0 0 1px #f59e0b, 0 4px 16px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`

## Shapes

With a roundedness level of `2`, the system relies on an approachable, protective curvature. Sharp 90-degree corners are eliminated to maintain a soft, non-hostile aesthetic suitable for child-protective environments, while avoiding childish or overly bulbous geometry.

### Radius System
- **Compact (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Checkboxes, small chips, form input fields, tooltips, and status tags.
- **Standard (`1rem` / 16px):** Telemetry panels, activity stream cards, dialog windows, and map control overlays.
- **Large (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Primary app containers, parent dashboards, and bottom navigation sheets.
- **Full Rounded (`9999px`):** Avatar indicators, SOS buttons, action pills, and status dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#182232` background with `#ffffff` text. `0.5rem` radius. `12px 20px` padding for default size. Transitions to `#0f172a` on hover.
- **Warning / Alert:** Solid `#f59e0b` background with `#182232` high-contrast text. Dedicated to urgent actions (e.g., "Send SOS Signal", "Lock Device").
- **Secondary:** Surface `#ffffff` with `1px solid #ded9cc` border and `#182232` text. Hovers to `#efece4`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, `#182232` text, `#efece4` hover fill.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Safe / Standard:** Background `#efece4`, text `#182232`, `4px 10px` padding, `9999px` pill radius, font `label-sm`.
- **Caution / Alert:** Background `#fef3c7`, text `#d97706`, border `1px solid #f59e0b`. Features a pulsating 6px circular dot for active events.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Background `#ffffff`, border `1.5px solid #ded9cc`, `0.5rem` radius, `12px 16px` inner padding.
- **Typography:** `body-md` in `#0f172a`. Placeholder in `#94a3b8`.
- **States:** Focus produces a crisp ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(24, 34, 50, 0.15)` with `#182232` border. In error/warning, shifts border to `#f59e0b` with `0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`.

### Cards & Activity Lists
- **Structure:** Crisp white background resting on the `#f7f5f0` canvas. Surrounded by a fine `1px solid #ded9cc` seam.
- **List Items:** Divided by `1px solid #efece4` lines. Hover states highlight via `#f7f5f0` transitions.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Unchecked:** `20px x 20px` box with `1.5px solid #ded9cc` on a `#ffffff` base. `4px` radius for checkbox, circular for radio.
- **Checked:** Filled with `#182232` exhibiting a pure white checkmark icon or center dot.

### Specialized Component: Child Status Safe-Node
- An integrated avatar, geofence status tag, and battery meter card.
- Features a left-border safety accent: 4px solid `#182232` (Normal), transitioning to `#f59e0b` (Caution/Alert).
- Quick-action buttons embedded directly into the header for one-tap calling and silent location pinging.
